What each role can do

Owner sees and changes everything, including this profile, spend categories and who can log in.

Manager sees all the numbers, adds and edits entries, and downloads reports and backups.

Staff logs in and sees only the screen for entering today's numbers. The database itself refuses to show staff anything else, so older days stay private even if someone tries.

Everyone logs in with their own email and password, on any phone or computer. Staff and managers do not need to share your login.
